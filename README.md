# hardop.

**Say it. See it.** — Speak, and watch your words become typography.

Hardop (Dutch for *"out loud"*) is a 24-hour app-challenge project by Max, Stijn & Caesar — the team behind [Back to Being](https://backtobeing.tech). Live speech transcription turns everything you say into animated, shareable typography. Built outdoors, on a park bench, dictated through smart glasses over Starlink.

## Run it

Open `index.html` in any browser. That's the whole site — one self-contained file, fonts embedded, works offline.

The live demo uses your browser's speech recognition (Chrome/Safari/Edge). Nothing you say leaves your device. Typing works everywhere as a fallback.

## Design

The visual identity follows the Back to Being house style: Instrument Serif with sage-green italic accents, Inter Tight, warm cream canvas with grain, and a living landscape in the five brand colours (sky, creek, sage, sand, tree).
